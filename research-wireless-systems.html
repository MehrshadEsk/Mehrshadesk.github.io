Wireless Communications
Networks.

Research background

My research in Wireless Communications Networks connects wireless sensor networks, radio-frequency systems, and intelligent 5G/6G radio access networks. I study reliable communications under changing coverage, mobility, interference, and energy constraints, using reinforcement learning, multi-agent control, and optimization to improve network decision-making. This includes energy-aware clustering and routing, autonomous coverage optimization, QoS-aware load balancing, and adaptive radio resource management. My RF-related work also examines electromagnetic-field interactions with biological systems. Across these topics, the aim is to design communication networks that are robust, efficient, and responsive to dynamic real-world conditions.

Research focus

01Sensor networks

Clustering, routing, and reliable sensing.

I study how reinforcement learning, multi-agent coordination, and energy-aware routing can balance traffic and extend the operating life of sensor networks.

02RF & communication systems

Connect communication theory to system design.

My work also examines radio-frequency systems, coverage, mobility, network reliability, and the interaction of electromagnetic fields with biological systems.

03Load balancing

Put capacity where it is needed.

I examine how reinforcement learning can optimize cell association and resource allocation when demand shifts and network observations are uncertain.

04Mobility management

Adapt to movement without losing stability.

I study policies that anticipate handovers and changing network pressure while balancing service quality, efficiency, and continuity.
