RESEARCH DIRECTION 1

Wireless Communications
Networks

Research background

This research direction brings together my work on wireless systems, RF foundations, radio access networks, and learning-driven network optimization. Across these projects, I study how communication systems can become more adaptive, reliable, and energy efficient under real deployment constraints. The common thread is the design of networked systems that can sense changing conditions, allocate resources effectively, and maintain quality of service from sensor networks to 5G radio environments.

Research focus

1Wireless systems

Communication architectures built for dynamic environments.

I study wireless sensor systems, RF-linked infrastructures, and communication models that support robust, scalable, and energy-aware operation.

2Radio access networks

Performance, mobility, and service quality in RANs.

My work examines load balancing, resource management, and coverage-related decision making in radio access networks under mobility and uncertainty.

3Optimization & learning

Learning-based control for network efficiency.

A major focus is the use of reinforcement learning and related optimization methods to improve adaptability, reliability, and end-to-end network performance.
