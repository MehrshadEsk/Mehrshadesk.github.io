Research background

My research in Wireless Networks and RF Systems focuses on developing intelligent, efficient, and reliable communication systems for increasingly dynamic wireless environments. My work spans wireless sensor networks, 5G radio access networks, RF electromagnetic fields, mobility and coverage optimization, energy-efficient routing, network reliability, and adaptive resource management. A central theme of my research is the integration of reinforcement learning, multi-agent systems, and optimization techniques with wireless communications to enable networks that can adapt autonomously to changing conditions. I have worked on problems ranging from autonomous coverage optimization in mobile wireless sensor networks to energy-aware routing, cluster-head selection, and power management for long-life sensor deployments. My RF-related research also extends to the study of radiofrequency electromagnetic fields and their interaction with biological systems. Through this work, I aim to bridge communication theory, intelligent network control, and practical system design, with a particular interest in technologies that can contribute to the evolution of next-generation wireless networks and IoT infrastructures.
