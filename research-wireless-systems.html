01What I work on

From individual sensors to complete wireless systems.

I have worked on wireless sensor networks, mobile sensor coverage, routing, cluster-head selection, energy management, network lifetime and reliability. My research uses game theory, reinforcement learning and multi-agent learning to improve network decisions under practical constraints such as limited energy, mobility and uncertain observations. My broader wireless work also includes 5G network configuration, mobility-aware load balancing and QoS-focused optimization.
