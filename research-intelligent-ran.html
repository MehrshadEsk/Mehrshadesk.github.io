Research background

My research in Artificial Intelligence for Radio Access Networks (AIRAN) focuses on using reinforcement learning and intelligent control to make wireless networks a little better at making decisions on their own. This work began with my undergraduate thesis at Iran University of Science and Technology (IUST), supervised by Dr. Hossein Soleimani, where I studied deep reinforcement learning for QoS-aware load balancing in 5G cellular networks under user mobility and uncertain channel conditions. Since then, my work has expanded into AI based coverage optimization, resource management, mobility, and multi-agent wireless systems. The broader goal is simple: help 5G and future 6G networks adapt to changing conditions without needing a human to babysit every decision.

Research focus

01Load balancing

Put capacity where it is needed.

I examine how reinforcement learning can optimize cell association and resource allocation when demand shifts and network observations are uncertain.

02Mobility management

Adapt to movement without losing stability.

I study policies that anticipate handovers and changing network pressure while balancing service quality, efficiency, and continuity.
