01What I work on

Making the radio access network more aware of what is happening around it.

My work in intelligent RAN is centered on AI-driven network control, with a particular focus on mobility, load balancing and quality of service. I have studied deep reinforcement learning for cellular load balancing under user mobility and incomplete observations, and my telecommunications experience has also included practical 3G, 4G and 5G network monitoring, configuration, optimization, neighboring-cell management and new-site deployment. The long-term direction is toward AI-native RAN architectures in which network decisions become more context-aware, adaptive and autonomous.
