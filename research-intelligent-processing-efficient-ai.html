[ R04 · INTELLIGENT PROCESSING AND EFFICIENT AI ]

Teach machines
to keep the useful bits.

My work here connects two signals that look very different on the surface: human voice and LLM context. The common problem is information efficiency — extracting what matters, remembering it intelligently, and adapting without throwing absurd amounts of data or compute at the problem.

01 / TWO SIGNALS

Different inputs.
Same obsession with efficiency.

Both research tracks ask how intelligent systems can preserve task-relevant information while operating with less data, less context, or tighter computational constraints.

01 · SPEECH INTELLIGENCE

Learn the speaker from the parts of speech that carry the most identity.

VowelVC focuses computation on vowel structure, combines learned vowel representations with a differentiable memory bank, and supports incremental few-shot speaker adaptation.

02 · EFFICIENT LANGUAGE AI

Keep the reasoning structure. Drop the context that is just taking up rent.

Graph-aware prompt compression treats reusable reasoning units as a structured graph and uses reinforcement learning to select compact contexts for black-box LLM inference.

THE THROUGH-LINE

Representation → selective memory → task-aware adaptation. Whether the raw input is a mel-spectrogram or a long reasoning prompt, the goal is to retain information that changes the answer and spend fewer resources on information that does not.
