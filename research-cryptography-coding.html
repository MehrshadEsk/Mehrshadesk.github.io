Secure Computing
and Processing.

Research background

My research in Secure Computing and Processing brings together cryptographic security, trustworthy computation, and efficient intelligent systems. On the security side, I investigate side-channel analysis, machine-learning-assisted key recovery, blockchain vulnerabilities, and the assumptions behind smart-contract randomness. On the processing side, I explore neural voice conversion, memory-augmented learning, few-shot adaptation, and reinforcement-learning-based compression for language models. The connecting question is how to make computational systems more secure, adaptive, and resource-efficient, while preserving the reliability of the information they process.

Research focus

01Cryptographic analysis

Learn from unintended physical leakage.

I investigate side-channel traces, deep-learning-assisted key recovery, and the gap between arithmetic correctness and resistance to physical observation.

02Blockchain security

Examine the assumptions behind trust.

I study how randomness and smart-contract design can introduce vulnerabilities, and what those weaknesses imply for secure decentralized systems.

03Speech intelligence

Adapt with limited examples and memory.

I explore neural voice conversion and incremental memory mechanisms that retain useful representations while adapting to new speakers from limited data.

04Efficient language AI

Keep the context that carries the reasoning.

I investigate graph-aware reinforcement learning for reusable context compression, balancing interpretability and reasoning quality with inference cost.
