01What I work on

Security viewed as both mathematics and a measurable system.

My work in cryptography and coding includes machine-learning-assisted side-channel analysis and smart-contract security. I have studied how deep-learning methods can recover cryptographic keys even when probabilistic countermeasures are used, and I have also worked on weaknesses in random-number generation inside Ethereum smart contracts. This area connects directly to my teaching background in cryptography and to my broader interest in robust communication systems, coding and intelligent security analysis.

SIDE CHANNELS

Cryptographic leakage

Analyzing measurable traces from cryptographic operations and learning patterns that reveal key information.

DEEP LEARNING

Key recovery

Using neural models to attack protected cryptographic implementations under probabilistic defenses.

SMART CONTRACTS

Randomness security

Studying exploitable weaknesses in random-number generation and state behavior in Ethereum contracts.
