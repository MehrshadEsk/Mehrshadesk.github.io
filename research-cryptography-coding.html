RESEARCH DIRECTION 2

Secure Computing
and Processing

Research background

This research direction combines my work in cybersecurity, cryptography, machine learning, and efficient computational processing. The projects gathered here focus on building trustworthy and capable computing systems, whether by improving the resilience of cryptographic implementations, studying smart-contract vulnerabilities, or designing learning systems that operate effectively under practical efficiency constraints. Together, they reflect an interest in both secure information handling and high-value intelligent processing.

Research focus

1Cybersecurity

Security analysis from protocols to practical attack surfaces.

I explore smart-contract vulnerabilities, side-channel analysis, and security-oriented evaluation of systems where implementation details matter as much as theory.

2Cryptography

Applied cryptographic reliability and key protection.

This line of work looks at how cryptographic mechanisms behave in real conditions and how machine learning can expose or help mitigate weaknesses.

3Processing & AI

Efficient learning and signal-aware computational models.

I also study machine learning systems for efficient processing, including neural methods for language and speech tasks where performance and resource use both matter.
