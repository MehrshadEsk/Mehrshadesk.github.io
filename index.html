01 / About

Research that moves from theory to systems.

My work sits at the intersection of communication networks and intelligent decision-making. I’m particularly interested in systems that can adapt to uncertainty, mobility, limited energy, and changing network conditions.

I earned my B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering, specializing in Telecommunication Systems, from the Iran University of Science and Technology. My undergraduate thesis explored deep reinforcement learning for QoS-aware load balancing in 5G cellular networks.
