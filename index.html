01 / About

Research that moves from theory to systems.

My work sits at the intersection of communication networks and intelligent decision-making. I am particularly interested in systems that can adapt to uncertainty, mobility, limited energy, complex infrastructure and changing network conditions.

My research spans wireless communications, 5G network optimization, wireless sensor networks, reinforcement learning, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, signal processing and machine-learning-driven network intelligence.

Beyond academic research, I have worked across telecommunications network operations, infrastructure project management, wireless sensor deployments and AI education.
