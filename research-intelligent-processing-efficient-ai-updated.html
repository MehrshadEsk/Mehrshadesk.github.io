Research background

My research in Intelligent Learning and Efficient Processing explores how AI systems can learn, adapt, and process information more efficiently, because making a model bigger is not always the most interesting solution. My work includes neural voice conversion, few-shot speaker adaptation, memory-augmented learning, and reinforcement-learning-based prompt compression for large language models. Across these projects, I’m interested in building AI systems that can do more with less: less data, less computation, less redundancy, and hopefully fewer GPUs begging for mercy. The broader goal is to develop intelligent models that are not only accurate, but also adaptive, efficient, and practical enough to work outside the lab.

Research focus

01Speech intelligence

Adapt with limited examples and memory.

I explore neural voice conversion and incremental memory mechanisms that retain useful representations while adapting to new speakers from limited data.

02Efficient language AI

Keep the context that carries the reasoning.

I investigate graph-aware reinforcement learning for reusable context compression, balancing interpretability and reasoning quality with inference cost.
